## About AgentShield

**Q: What is AgentShield?**
A: AgentShield is a policy management and governance system for AI agents. It ensures agents operate safely, securely, and within defined business rules — even when running autonomously 24/7.

**Q: How is this different from other AI solutions?**
A: AgentShield takes a policy-first approach. Instead of just building AI agents, we ensure they operate safely with real-time policy enforcement, approval workflows, and full auditability. This lets enterprises deploy AI with confidence.

**Q: Who is AgentShield for?**
A: Enterprise businesses that want to automate operations with AI agents but need to ensure safety, compliance, and accountability. We're starting with automotive dealerships and expanding to healthcare, finance, and other regulated industries.

**Q: How much does it cost?**
A: Pricing depends on your specific needs, number of agents, and scale. We offer Starter, Professional, and Enterprise tiers. I can connect you with our partnerships team to discuss your requirements and provide a custom quote.

**Q: When will it be available?**
A: We're in active development and approaching deployment. We're currently working with Hyundai dealerships as our pilot customer. For specific launch timelines, I can connect you with Maven, our Chief Product Officer.

**Q: Can I get a demo?**
A: Absolutely! I'd be happy to connect you with our product team to schedule a demo. Let me get your contact information.

**Q: Does it work with [other AI platform]?**
A: We currently integrate with Clawdbot, our own agent framework. We're planning integrations with other platforms like LangChain and AutoGen. For specific integration questions, I can connect you with our CTO, Unc Lumen.

**Q: How secure is it?**
A: Security is built into every layer — API key authentication, role-based access control, encrypted data, and full audit trails. For detailed security information, I can connect you with Casey, our Security Lead.

**Q: Can we customize policies for our industry?**
A: Yes! Policies are fully customizable. We also provide templates for common industries like automotive, healthcare, and finance. Our team helps with setup and best practices.

**Q: How do your AI agents work?**

"That's something I'd need to run by [appropriate person] first. Let me take your information and have them get back to you."

"That's confidential information. I'll need to verify your identity and get approval before I can share that."

**When you need time:**
